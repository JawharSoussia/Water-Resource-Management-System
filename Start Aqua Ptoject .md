## 🔄 What the AQUA Project Looks Like

### The Data Flow
```
NASA SMAP Satellite (Free!)
├─ Soil moisture (0-100%)
├─ Daily updates
└─ Entire Earth coverage

    ↓

EU Copernicus Satellite (Free!)
├─ Crop health (NDVI)
├─ 5-day updates
└─ Field-level resolution

    ↓

NOAA Weather (Free!)
├─ Temperature forecast
├─ Rain prediction
└─ Wind/humidity

    ↓

Your Pipeline (MinIO + PostgreSQL)
├─ Store raw satellite data
├─ Calculate water stress index
├─ Predict irrigation needs
└─ Generate alerts

    ↓

Farmer Dashboard (Streamlit)
├─ "Should I water today?" → YES/NO
├─ Water savings ($)
├─ Yield forecast
└─ Mobile accessible

    ↓

Government Dashboard (Streamlit + Plotly)
├─ Regional water status map
├─ Groundwater level decline
├─ Drought early warning
└─ UN/World Bank reports
```
